1. Fundamentals

Order vs. Degree

Order: The order of the highest derivative present in the equation.

Degree: The power of the highest derivative, provided the equation is a polynomial in derivatives (no radicals or fractions on derivatives).

Example: \( \left( \frac{d^2y}{dx^2} \right)^{3/2} = 4 + \left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^{1/2} \)
How to find Degree
1. Remove radicals/fractions on derivatives.
2. Square both sides to clear the 1/2 power: \( \dots = (4 + y')^2 \)
3. Order is 2. Degree is the power of \(y''\) after clearing.

2. First Order Differential Equations

General Form: \( \frac{dy}{dx} = f(x, y) \)

graph TD A[Start: dy/dx = f] --> B{Separable?} B -- Yes --> C[Integrate f] B -- No --> D{Homogeneous?} D -- Yes --> E[Put y = vx] D -- No --> F{Linear?} F -- Yes --> G[Integrating Factor] F -- No --> H{Exact?} H -- Yes --> I[Solution Formula] H -- No --> J[Reducible to Linear/Exact]

A. Variable Separable

Can you write it as \( g(y) dy = h(x) dx \)?

Method: Integrate both sides: \( \int g(y) dy = \int h(x) dx + C \)

B. Homogeneous Equations

Function \( f(x,y) \) is homogeneous if \( f(kx, ky) = k^n f(x,y) \).

Form: \( \frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{g(x,y)}{h(x,y)} \) where g and h are same degree.

Method: Substitute \( y = vx \), then \( \frac{dy}{dx} = v + x\frac{dv}{dx} \). Result becomes separable.

C. Linear Differential Equation (LDE)

Standard Form: \( \frac{dy}{dx} + P(x)y = Q(x) \)

Integrating Factor (I.F.): \( e^{\int P dx} \)
Solution: \( y \cdot (\text{I.F.}) = \int Q \cdot (\text{I.F.}) dx + C \)
Bernoulli's Equation (Reducible to Linear)
Form: \( \frac{dy}{dx} + Py = Qy^n \)
1. Divide by \( y^n \).
2. Substitute \( v = y^{1-n} \).
3. Equation becomes Linear in \( v \).

D. Exact Differential Equations

Form: \( M(x,y)dx + N(x,y)dy = 0 \)

Condition: \( \frac{\partial M}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial N}{\partial x} \)

Solution: \( \int_{y=\text{const}} M dx + \int (\text{terms of N free from x}) dy = C \)

3. Higher Order Linear ODEs

Form: \( \frac{d^ny}{dx^n} + k_1 \frac{d^{n-1}y}{dx^{n-1}} + \dots + k_n y = X(x) \) (Constant Coefficients)

Complete Solution: \( y = \text{Complementary Function (CF)} + \text{Particular Integral (PI)} \)

I. Complementary Function (CF)

Solve Auxiliary Equation: \( D^n + k_1 D^{n-1} + \dots + k_n = 0 \)

Roots Nature Roots Complementary Function (CF)
Real & Distinct \( m_1, m_2 \) \( c_1 e^{m_1 x} + c_2 e^{m_2 x} \)
Real & Equal \( m, m \) \( (c_1 + c_2 x) e^{mx} \)
Complex Pair \( \alpha \pm i\beta \) \( e^{\alpha x} (c_1 \cos \beta x + c_2 \sin \beta x) \)

II. Particular Integral (PI)

Formula: \( \text{PI} = \frac{1}{f(D)} X(x) \)

graph LR A[Type of X] --> B{e^ax} A --> C{sin/cos ax} A --> D{x^m} B --> B1[Put D = a] C --> C1[Put D^2 = -a^2] D --> D1[Expand (1+f(D))^-1]
Detailed Cases for PI
  • \( X = e^{ax} \): Put \( D = a \). If \( f(a)=0 \), PI \( = x \frac{1}{f'(a)} e^{ax} \).
  • \( X = \sin(ax+b) \): Put \( D^2 = -a^2 \). If denominator zero, differentiate wrt D and multiply by x.
  • \( X = x^m \): Convert to \( [1 + \phi(D)]^{-1} \) and expand using binomial theorem.
  • \( X = e^{ax} V(x) \): \( \frac{1}{f(D)} e^{ax}V = e^{ax} \frac{1}{f(D+a)} V \).

III. Variation of Parameters

Used for finding PI when standard methods fail (e.g., \( X = \tan x \)).

\( \text{PI} = -y_1 \int \frac{y_2 X}{W} dx + y_2 \int \frac{y_1 X}{W} dx \)
Where \( W \) (Wronskian) = \( \begin{vmatrix} y_1 & y_2 \\ y_1' & y_2' \end{vmatrix} \)

4. Cauchy-Euler Equation

Linear DE with variable coefficients.

Form: \( x^2 \frac{d^2y}{dx^2} + ax \frac{dy}{dx} + by = Q(x) \)

Transformation:

  • Put \( x = e^t \) or \( t = \ln x \).
  • \( x \frac{dy}{dx} \rightarrow D_t y \)
  • \( x^2 \frac{d^2y}{dx^2} \rightarrow D_t(D_t - 1) y \)

This converts it into a Linear DE with constant coefficients.

5. Partial Differential Equations (PDE)

Classification of 2nd Order PDE

Standard Form: \( A \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} + B \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x \partial y} + C \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} + \dots = 0 \)

Condition (\( B^2 - 4AC \)) Type Example
\( < 0 \) Elliptic Laplace Eqn
\( = 0 \) Parabolic Heat Eqn
\( > 0 \) Hyperbolic Wave Eqn

Method of Separation of Variables

Assume solution \( u(x,t) = X(x) \cdot T(t) \).

Substitute back into PDE to separate terms of \( x \) and \( t \).

1D Wave Equation: \( \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} = c^2 \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} \)
1D Heat Equation: \( \frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = c^2 \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} \)
2D Laplace Equation: \( \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} = 0 \)

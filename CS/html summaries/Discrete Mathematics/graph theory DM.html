1. Fundamentals & Degree Sequence

Handshaking Lemma

The sum of degrees of all vertices is twice the number of edges.

$$ \sum_{v \in V} \deg(v) = 2|E| $$

Corollary: The number of odd degree vertices in any graph must be even.

Havel-Hakimi Algorithm

Used to determine if a sequence of integers is graphic (can form a simple graph).

1. Sort sequence (descending).
2. Remove first element (k).
3. Subtract 1 from next k elements.
4. Repeat. 
   - If negative number appears: Invalid.
   - If all zeros: Valid.

Simple Graph Constraints

For a simple graph with $n$ vertices:

  • Max Degree: $n-1$
  • Max Edges: $n(n-1)/2$
  • Min Edges (Connected): $n-1$

3. Connectivity

Vertex vs Edge Connectivity

Vertex Connectivity ($\kappa(G)$): Min vertices to remove to disconnect graph.

Edge Connectivity ($\lambda(G)$): Min edges to remove to disconnect graph.

$$ \kappa(G) \le \lambda(G) \le \delta(G) $$ Where $\delta(G)$ is min degree.

Cut Sets

  • Cut Edge (Bridge): Removal increases components. Cannot be part of a cycle.
  • Cut Vertex (Articulation Point): Removal increases components.

Inequality Theorem

For a graph with $n$ vertices and $k$ components, edges $e$ satisfy:

$$ n-k \le e \le \frac{(n-k)(n-k+1)}{2} $$

4. Operations & Isomorphism

Isomorphism Conditions

Two graphs $G_1$ and $G_2$ are isomorphic if:

  1. Same number of vertices $|V_1| = |V_2|$
  2. Same number of edges $|E_1| = |E_2|$
  3. Same degree sequence
  4. Same cycle lengths (structural check)

Complement Graph ($\bar{G}$)

Edges present in $K_n$ but missing in $G$.

$$ |E(G)| + |E(\bar{G})| = \frac{n(n-1)}{2} $$

Self-Complementary: $G \cong \bar{G}$. Requires $n \equiv 0,1 \pmod 4$.

Graph Operations

  • Union ($G_1 \cup G_2$): $V = V_1 \cup V_2$, $E = E_1 \cup E_2$
  • Intersection ($G_1 \cap G_2$): Common vertices and edges.
  • Ring Sum ($G_1 \oplus G_2$): Edges in either, but not both ($E_1 \Delta E_2$). No common edges.

6. Planarity

Euler's Formula

For a connected planar graph:

$$ v - e + r = 2 $$

Where $r$ is regions (faces) including the infinite one.

Sum of degrees of regions: $\sum \deg(r_i) = 2e$

Inequalities (Necessary Conditions)

If $G$ is planar ($v \ge 3$):

  1. $$ e \le 3v - 6 $$
  2. If triangle-free (no $C_3$): $$ e \le 2v - 4 $$
  3. Min degree $\delta(G) \le 5$

Kuratowski's Theorem

A graph is non-planar if and only if it contains a subgraph homeomorphic to:

  • $K_5$ (Complete graph, 5 vertices)
  • $K_{3,3}$ (Utility graph, bipartite)

7. Graph Coloring

Chromatic Number $\chi(G)$: Min colors needed such that no two adjacent vertices share a color.

Common Values

  • $K_n \to n$
  • $C_n \to$ 2 (if $n$ even), 3 (if $n$ odd)
  • $W_n \to$ 3 (if rim even), 4 (if rim odd)
  • Bipartite $\to 2$
  • Planar Graph $\to \le 4$ (4-color theorem)

Polynomials (Introduction)

Ways to color graph with $\lambda$ colors: $P(G, \lambda)$.

For Tree: $\lambda(\lambda-1)^{n-1}$

For $K_n$: $\lambda(\lambda-1)...(\lambda-n+1)$

8. Independence, Matching & Covering

Definitions relating to sets of Vertices (V) and Edges (E).

Concept Symbol Definition Relationship
Vertex Covering $\alpha$ or $\beta$ Min vertices to cover all edges. $\alpha + \beta = n$
(Gallai Identity for Vertices)
Independent Set $\beta$ or $\alpha$ Max vertices with no edges between them.
Edge Covering $\rho$ or $\alpha'$ Min edges to cover all vertices. $m + \rho = n$
(If no isolated vertices)
Matching $m$ or $\beta'$ Max edges with no common vertices.

Definitions

  • Maximal: Cannot add more items (local max).
  • Maximum: Largest possible set in the graph (global max).
  • Minimal: Cannot remove items (local min).
  • Minimum: Smallest possible set (global min).

Matching in Special Graphs

Matching Number $m(G)$:

  • $K_n \to \lfloor n/2 \rfloor$
  • $C_n \to \lfloor n/2 \rfloor$
  • $K_{m,n} \to \min(m, n)$

Perfect Matching: Matches all vertices (Requires $|V|$ even).

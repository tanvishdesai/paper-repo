3. Ordering & Ranking

The Golden Formula

For a single person in a row:

$$ \text{Total Persons (T)} = \text{Left (L)} + \text{Right (R)} - 1 $$

Derived Formulas

  • Find Position from Right: \( R = T - L + 1 \)
  • Find Position from Left: \( L = T - R + 1 \)

Swapping Positions

If Person A shifts from \(L_1\) to \(L_2\) and Person B is at \(R_1\), and they calculate the total based on the new arrangement, always map the new position of one to the old position of the other depending on the overlap context.

Dice Rules

Standard Dice

Sum of opposite faces is always 7.

  • 1 ↔ 6
  • 2 ↔ 5
  • 3 ↔ 4

General Dice

Sum of adjacent faces can be 7.

Rotation Rule: If one number is common in two views, write numbers clockwise starting from the common number to find opposites.

Open Dice Strategy

In an unfolded dice (cross shape or T-shape), alternate faces are opposite to each other.

6. Coding & Decoding

Common Patterns

  1. Position Shifting: Forward (+1, +2) or Backward (-1, -2).
  2. Opposite Pairs: A↔Z, B↔Y, C↔X (Tip: "AZ", "BY", "CX" "DW" "EV").
  3. Number Coding: Sum of position values (e.g., CAT = 3+1+20 = 24).
  4. Chinese Coding: Comparing common words in sentences to find code equivalents.
EJOTY Rule: Remember position multiples of 5.
E=5, J=10, O=15, T=20, Y=25.

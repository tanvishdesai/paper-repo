Trailing Zeroes & Divisibility

Trailing Zeroes in n!: Count the number of 5s in prime factorization.

$$ Number\ of\ Zeroes = \lfloor \frac{n}{5} \rfloor + \lfloor \frac{n}{25} \rfloor + \lfloor \frac{n}{125} \rfloor + ... $$

Sum of N Natural Numbers: $$ \sum n = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} $$

Average of Natural Numbers: $$ \frac{n+1}{2} $$

Successive Change

For two successive changes of $a\%$ and $b\%$:

$$ Net\% = a + b + \frac{a \times b}{100} $$

Note: Use positive for increase, negative for decrease.

Average Speed: If distances are equal, $$ S_{avg} = \frac{2xy}{x+y} $$

3. Profit, Loss & Discount

Core Concepts Flow

graph LR CP[Cost Price] -- Profit/Loss % --> SP[Selling Price] MP[Marked Price] -- Discount % --> SP CP -- Markup % --> MP

Formulas

  • $$ Profit\% = \frac{SP-CP}{CP} \times 100 $$
  • $$ Loss\% = \frac{CP-SP}{CP} \times 100 $$
  • $$ Discount\% = \frac{MP-SP}{MP} \times 100 $$

Dishonest Dealer

If a dealer uses false weight (e.g., 900g instead of 1kg):

$$ Profit\% = \frac{Error}{True\ Value - Error} \times 100 $$

Example: Uses 900g for 1kg $$ \frac{100}{900} \times 100 = 11.11\% $$

Rule of Alligation (Mixtures)

Used when mixing two ingredients to get a target average price.

Ratio (Cheaper : Dearer) = (D - M) : (M - C)

4. Time & Work

The LCM Method

To solve work problems without fractions:

  1. Assume Total Work = LCM of days taken by individuals.
  2. Calculate Efficiency (Work/Day) for each person.
  3. $$ Time = \frac{Total\ Work}{Total\ Efficiency} $$

Chain Rule (MDH)

Comparing two scenarios:

$$ \frac{M_1 D_1 H_1}{W_1} = \frac{M_2 D_2 H_2}{W_2} $$
M=Men, D=Days, H=Hours, W=Work

Pipes & Cisterns

  • Inlet Pipe: Positive Efficiency (+)
  • Outlet Pipe: Negative Efficiency (-)
  • Net Work = (Inlet Eff - Outlet Eff) × Time

5. Time, Speed & Distance

Relative Speed

Boats & Streams

Let $B$ = Boat Speed, $S$ = Stream Speed

  • Downstream (with flow): $$ D = B + S $$
  • Upstream (against flow): $$ U = B - S $$
  • $$ B = \frac{D+U}{2}, \quad S = \frac{D-U}{2} $$
Trains crossing objects:
  • Crossing Pole/Man: Distance = Length of Train ($L_T$)
  • Crossing Platform/Bridge: Distance = $L_T + L_{Platform}$
  • Crossing Another Train: Distance = $L_{T1} + L_{T2}$ (Always add lengths)

6. Permutations & Combinations

Factorial Basics

  • $n! = n \times (n-1) \times ... \times 1$
  • $0! = 1$
  • $5! = 120$

Arrangements (Permutation)

Arranging $r$ items from $n$ distinct items:

$$ ^nP_r = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!} $$

Circular Arrangement: $(n-1)!$

Selection (Combination)

Selecting $r$ items from $n$ distinct items (Order doesn't matter):

$$ ^nC_r = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!} $$

7. Clocks & Calendars

Clocks: Angle Formula

Angle $\theta$ between Hour ($H$) and Minute ($M$) hand:

$$ \theta = |30H - \frac{11}{2}M| $$
  • Coincide (0°): 11 times in 12 hrs.
  • Opposite (180°): 11 times in 12 hrs.
  • Right Angle (90°): 22 times in 12 hrs.

Calendars: Odd Days

Odd Days = Remainder of Days / 7

Normal Year (365)1 Odd Day
Leap Year (366)2 Odd Days
100 Years5 Odd Days
400 Years0 Odd Days

Leap Year Rule: Divisible by 4 (but for centuries, must be divisible by 400).

8. Mensuration & Data Interpretation

2D Areas

  • Circle: $\pi r^2$
  • Sector: $\frac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2$
  • Trapezium: $\frac{1}{2}(a+b)h$
  • Equilateral $\Delta$: $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^2$

3D Volumes

  • Cylinder: $\pi r^2 h$
  • Cone: $\frac{1}{3} \pi r^2 h$
  • Sphere: $\frac{4}{3} \pi r^3$
  • Hemisphere: $\frac{2}{3} \pi r^3$

Surface Areas (Total)

  • Cylinder: $2\pi r(h+r)$
  • Cone: $\pi r(l+r)$
  • Sphere: $4\pi r^2$

Data Interpretation Types

  • Pie Chart: Values in degrees ($Total = 360^{\circ}$) or Percentages ($Total = 100\%$). Conversion: $1\% = 3.6^{\circ}$.
  • Bar/Line Graph: Look for trends (slope) and absolute values.
  • Table: Often involves comparing ratios or percentages across rows/columns.
